The admin dashboard has been rebuilt around clearer, more glanceable cards.

- **New overview row:** Present, on time, late, and not checked in now sit in one summary strip with small trend lines, plus a punctuality score dial and an average check-in time for the day
- **Arrival heatmap:** Check-ins are charted by weekday and hour, so managers can instantly see the busiest arrival windows across the period
- **Live activity feed:** The latest check-ins and check-outs appear as a rolling timeline with employee names, times, and locations
- **Late ranking:** The five employees with the most late check-ins are ranked with total minutes late, each linking straight to their attendance history
- **Cleaner layout:** Collapsed sections and the manual punch shortcut are gone; filters now live behind a single button and every card links to its drill-down page

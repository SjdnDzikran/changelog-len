Confirming a pickup now produces the same kind of evidence as delivery, protecting the company against disputes about whether and where goods were collected.

- **Dedicated confirmation page:** Confirming a pickup opens a full page instead of a small popup, showing booking details, notes and evidence in one place
- **Stamped photo evidence:** Up to five photos can be attached, each automatically stamped with the location and time it was taken, the same as delivery proof
- **GPS location captured:** The pickup's coordinates and resolved address are saved with the status and shown later in the booking's status history, alongside thumbnail photos that open full size
- **Reliable GPS:** When a courier switches on location services during confirmation, the app allows a longer moment for the first GPS fix instead of failing immediately

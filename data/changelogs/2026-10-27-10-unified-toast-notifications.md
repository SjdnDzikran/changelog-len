- **One notification style everywhere:** All confirmations and errors, from saving profile changes to approving leave and downloading PDFs, now appear as compact pop-up notifications in the top-right corner, replacing the scattered red boxes and success banners that used to appear inside each form.
- **Progress feedback:** Long actions like saving, signing out, or generating a report show a "working" notification that turns into a confirmation or an error message when finished, so it is always clear whether an action succeeded.
- **Clearer errors on punches:** Failed attendance punches and manual punch conflicts now show the exact server reason as a notification, in addition to the on-screen guidance for recovering from uncertain punches.

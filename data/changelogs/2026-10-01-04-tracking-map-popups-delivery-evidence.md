Clicking a dot on the tracking map used to take several tries. The pins now have a much larger invisible tap area, so one tap is enough even when several pins sit close together, and the keyboard works on them too.

- **Delivery proof on the map:** Tapping a delivered parcel's pin shows who received it, when, any notes, and up to three photos of the handover, without leaving the map
- **Quick AWB shortcut:** Every parcel popup has a direct button to the AWB's status screen, so updating a shipment after a failed attempt takes one tap
- **Bigger tap targets:** The visible dot stays small and tidy, but a 44-pixel invisible area around it makes pins far easier to hit on touch screens
- **Multiple parcels at one address:** When several shipments share the same drop point, the popup offers a picker so each one can still be opened individually

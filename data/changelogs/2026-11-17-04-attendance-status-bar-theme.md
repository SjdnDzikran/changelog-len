The phone status bar in the installed Attendance app no longer flashes white when the app is set to dark mode.

- **Theme-matched status bar:** The status bar color now follows the app's light or dark theme instead of always staying white, with text/icons switching contrast accordingly
- **Respects the in-app choice:** A user's theme selection inside the app overrides the phone's system setting, and the color also applies to the offline fallback page

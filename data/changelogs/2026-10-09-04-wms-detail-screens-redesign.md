Following the main refresh, all 36 detail, action and management screens were redesigned to match, covering inbound and outbound detail for every business type, delivery, adjustments, transfers, serial numbers and more.

- **Workflow progress at a glance:** Inbound, outbound and change-material detail pages now open with a progress strip showing exactly which stage the document is at (Draft, Staged, Confirmed, Allocated) and a plain-language note of what happens next, so staff no longer guess where a document stands
- **Cleaner page structure:** Every detail page now has a clear header with a back link, the document number with its status, key facts (date, item count, related document numbers), and the action buttons grouped in one place
- **Readable forms and item lists:** Document fields sit in a tidy auto-fitting grid, and item lists merge related columns into compact cells with chips instead of wide colored rows
- **Fully themed pop-ups:** Every modal and input field now follows the Light and Dark themes, including date pickers and read-only view screens, instead of staying light-only
- **Clearer test results:** Confirm-on-test shows serial number matches and mismatches as green and terracotta chips (mismatches get an alert icon) instead of hard-to-read bright labels

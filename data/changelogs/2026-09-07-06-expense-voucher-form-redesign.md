The petty cash expense voucher form was reorganized into clearly grouped sections with much easier money input.

- **Grouped sections:** Trip identity, odometer and fuel readings, road money and driver incentives, operational costs, and e-toll each get their own labelled section, with the Total in a highlighted panel of its own so it is impossible to miss.
- **Thousands separators while typing:** Money fields format themselves as you type (for example 1.500.000), so amounts stay readable and mis-keyed digits stand out immediately.
- **Familiar field layout:** Labels now sit above each field with rupiah and kilometre markers attached, matching the rest of the app's forms and making the voucher quicker to fill in on the road.

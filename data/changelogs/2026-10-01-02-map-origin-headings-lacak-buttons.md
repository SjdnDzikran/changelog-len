Three refinements to how shipment tracking reads at a glance: the start of a trip is no longer mistaken for a delivered parcel, page titles take less space, and the tracking buttons are easier to spot.

- **Origin marker fixed:** The start of a trip (pickup point or departure branch) is now a dark blue badge with a warehouse or shop icon; it used to be green, the colour that everywhere else means delivered
- **Same legend everywhere:** The map legend is now a shared fold-away card used on Live Monitoring and on every tracking map, always listing only the marks actually on the map
- **Compact page headings:** The title banners across Manifest, DRS and AWB pages were rebuilt as one shared design, giving every page more usable space
- **Bolder Lacak buttons:** The Lacak and Update buttons on monitoring cards are now filled pill buttons that are easier to find and tap

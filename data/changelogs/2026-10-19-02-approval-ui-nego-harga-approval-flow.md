Price negotiation information is now visible directly inside the approval screens, so approvers no longer have to hunt for it.

- **Price summary in the Approve dialog:** For BL Outbound forms, the final approval confirmation now shows the compact CJ reference price next to the agreed price before confirming
- **Direct Nego Harga action:** Forms waiting at the Nego Harga step get a dedicated "Nego Harga" button on desktop rows and mobile cards in Need Approval, plus a "Nego" badge next to the step name
- **Richer price comparison:** The price panel shows coverage and difference-percentage pills, a per-route tariff column (charter or per-kg rate, plus wooden packing), and a quick "Salin angka referensi" button
- **Step types in Flow Configuration:** Each flow step now displays its type: Approval, Data Entry (AWB), or Nego Harga
- **More useful Users page:** User rows and cards now show WhatsApp numbers (with a Dummy badge for placeholder numbers) and group memberships, and search matches phone numbers and group names too

- **Installable on phones and desktops:** The attendance web app can now be installed to the home screen (Android/iOS) or as a desktop app, launching full-screen like a native app.
- **Offline fallback page:** When the connection drops while navigating, a clear bilingual (Indonesian/English) offline page appears with a retry button, instead of a blank error screen.
- **Strict privacy for offline mode:** Only the offline page itself is stored on the device. Attendance records, photos, payslips and all other data are never cached, and attendance punches always require a live connection.
- **Calmer updates:** New app versions wait until all windows are closed before applying, so an in-progress punch or form is never interrupted by a forced reload.

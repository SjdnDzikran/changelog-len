The Add, Edit and Detail form pages (AWB, Booking, Warehouse, Invoice and others) previously had plain white title bars that did not match the purple banners used on list pages. Every form page now opens with the same compact purple banner, so the whole application looks like one product.

- **Consistent look:** All form pages share one banner design, matching the list pages that were updated earlier
- **More room for content:** The compact banner starts content higher on the screen, so less scrolling is needed to reach the actual form
- **Clear main action:** On the banner, the back button is subtle while the page's main action (Edit, Export, View Status) stands out in solid white, so there is always one obvious next step
- **Better on mobile:** The wide item tables on the AWB request form now scroll sideways inside their own card instead of stretching the whole page

- **No more scroll bounce:** Pages stop overscrolling when you reach the top or bottom, so long lists no longer judder on touch screens and trackpads.
- **Scrolling over tables restored:** Scrolling with the mouse wheel or a swipe now works when the cursor is over a data table, instead of getting stuck. Horizontal table scrolling still works as before.
- **Refreshed pop-up dialogs:** View and edit dialogs got a cleaner look with properly aligned titles, footers, and buttons, so actions like "Tutup" and "Simpan" sit where you expect them.
- **Polished form fields:** Input boxes and dropdowns now have consistent heights, borders, and focus highlighting, and dropdown fields show a clear arrow icon.
- **Select and click in tables:** Cell text in tables can be selected and copied again, and document numbers (DO, AWB, adjustment, transfer numbers) are now clearly clickable links that open the detail page.

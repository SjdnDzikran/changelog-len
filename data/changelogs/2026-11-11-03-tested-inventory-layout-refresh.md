All four Tested Inventory tabs share a tighter, more consistent layout on desktop and mobile.

- **One compact toolbar:** Search, date range, Refresh, Excel download, Resolve, and Test Inventory actions now sit together in a single tidy toolbar on the Rekap, Per Material, Per S/N, and Approval tabs, instead of being scattered across separate rows
- **Nested frame removed:** The extra card-inside-a-card frame around each tab is gone, giving tables more breathing room
- **Status filters tucked away:** Status checkboxes moved into a compact second row that follows the active light or dark theme and collapses behind a Filters button on phones
- **Small screens handled:** Controls wrap neatly at narrow widths and dates stay readable on phones

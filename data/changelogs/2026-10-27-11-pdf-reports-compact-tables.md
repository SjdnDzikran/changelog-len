- **Spreadsheet-style layout:** Downloaded PDF reports (daily attendance, leave, permits, visits, overtime, work reports) are now landscape tables with one row per entry, matching the familiar old print sheets, instead of one page per record.
- **Monthly attendance matrix:** The monthly team report is back to the classic grid: one row per employee with a status column for every calendar day (H hadir, T terlambat, A alpha, C cuti, I izin), a legend, holiday notes and automatic totals per person.
- **Everything fits on fewer pages:** Compact rows and repeated table headers mean typical daily and monthly reports fit on a single page, and long descriptions flow across pages without losing a single sentence.
- **Payslips stay statements:** The payslip keeps its portrait one-page statement look with a clear component and amount table, and a payroll run list stays a table even when it holds just one run.

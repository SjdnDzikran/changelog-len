Employees without a profile photo get a generated colorful avatar, and those avatars now subtly move.

- **Gently animated avatars:** The generated avatars in the sidebar and on the profile page now breathe, bob, and blink softly instead of sitting frozen as static images.
- **Fixes avatar sizing:** Animated avatars in the sidebar were being squeezed down to icon size; they now keep the correct photo-style dimensions wherever they appear.

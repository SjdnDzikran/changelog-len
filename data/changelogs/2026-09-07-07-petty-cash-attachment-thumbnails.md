The petty cash list now shows what was attached instead of only file names.

- **Photo thumbnails:** Image attachments appear as small photo previews directly in the list, so receipts and proof documents are recognizable at a glance.
- **Clear document icons:** PDFs and other files get a dedicated document icon instead of a plain text chip.
- **Click to view:** Any thumbnail or icon opens the full attachment preview, just like before.

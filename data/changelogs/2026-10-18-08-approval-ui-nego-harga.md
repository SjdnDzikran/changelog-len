The approval app now supports the new Nego Harga step and shows CJ prices side by side on BL Outbound forms.

- **Nego Harga in Need Approval:** When a form reaches the price negotiation step, the action menu offers recording the price instead of a plain approve, with a dialog showing the CJ reference per route
- **Enter or accept a price:** The negotiator types the agreed total (with a live difference against the reference shown while typing) or takes the reference price in one click when all routes are priced
- **Harga CJ card:** Every submitted BL Outbound form shows the reference quote, the agreed price, who agreed it, when, and any note
- **Stays in sync:** After a price is recorded from a WhatsApp link, refreshing the card picks up the agreement

The attendance app now has its own app icon instead of reusing the company logo, used everywhere from the login page to installed-home-screen shortcuts and printed reports.

- **Dedicated icon:** A location pin with a clock face, drawn in LEN blue, replaces the old company wordmark as the app's identity on login, the employee home screen, the admin sidebar, and browser and phone home-screen icons
- **Playful touch:** On hover the pin hops and the clock's minute hand sweeps one full turn, giving the brand a bit of life without slowing anything down
- **Adapts to dark mode:** The icon automatically switches to a navy variant on dark backgrounds so it stays crisp everywhere
- **Consistent reports:** PDF reports now carry the new icon instead of the old logo file

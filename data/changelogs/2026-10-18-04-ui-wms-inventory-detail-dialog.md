The pop-up screens warehouse staff use to inspect inventory were rebuilt into one modern, consistent dialog across all inventory types.

- **One dialog for everything:** Network, BNPB, XL Home, and all seven Channel products now share the same detail view for stock, status history, and serial numbers, instead of four separate older screens
- **Filter chips:** Searchable filters for location, unit, vendor, status, and user, with removable chips and a one-click reset, make it easy to drill down and back out
- **Readable tables:** Related information is combined into fewer, well-organized columns, so stock levels and history are easier to scan at a glance
- **Smooth navigation:** Moving from stock to history or serial numbers and back keeps your filters and page exactly where you left them
- **Works on phones:** Controls are sized and laid out for touch, and both Light and Dark themes are supported

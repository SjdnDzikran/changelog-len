**Feels like an installed app:** The employee attendance experience now looks and behaves like a native Android app: a top bar with the screen title, a back arrow on deeper pages, and a floating bottom navigation bar with Home and Profile.

**One-tap check-in camera:** Tapping the big Check in button opens a full-screen camera immediately, like a phone's camera app. Location is read automatically in the background and shown as a small accuracy badge, a large shutter button captures the selfie, and submission returns straight home. The old multi-step form with notes and extra presses is gone.

**Simpler home screen:** The home page greets the employee by name with today's date, shows check-in and check-out times as large cards with the scheduled shift, and puts the three most-used requests (leave, permit, overtime) as colored quick-action tiles, with field work (visits, reports) in a grouped list below.

**Profile as a settings page:** The profile page is now a phone-style settings list: identity card with photo change button, account links, dark-mode toggle, language picker, and sign out, replacing the previous grid of buttons. A proper confirmation dialog now appears when leaving a page with unsaved changes instead of a browser popup.

**Company letterhead on every report:** All exported PDFs (daily attendance, monthly team recap, leave, permits, visits, overtime, work reports, payroll) now print in portrait on official letterhead with the LEN Logistics company name and logo, replacing the plain blue-bordered generic sheets.

**Columns match the legacy paper forms:** Each report type now prints exactly the fields of its corresponding legacy sheet, arranged the same way: employee name and position in one cell, schedule and punch times together, durations written as "8 jam 58 menit", payroll split into earnings and deductions columns. Internal IDs and technical references never reach the paper.

**Readable dates and totals:** Dates print as "15 Oct 2026" instead of raw codes, months as "Oktober 2026", and work durations as hours and minutes. The monthly team recap keeps its one-row-per-employee calendar grid with holiday highlighting and a legend.

**Evidence photos embedded:** Check-in and check-out selfies, visit photos, and work report attachments are embedded as images directly in the report rows. Photos that are no longer available print a clear note instead of a broken link.

**Payroll payslips as statements:** Payslips print as a proper salary statement: earnings grouped and totaled, deductions itemized (BPJS, absence, late arrivals) with their calculation basis, and take-home pay highlighted, with a note that system-generated payslips are valid without signature.

Collapsible filters now open and close with a gentle slide, and the password page confirms what happened after saving.

- **Animated mobile filters:** The collapsible Filters panel on all ten inbound and outbound monitoring screens, plus the serial-detail material and location summary, now opens and closes with a smooth slide and a rotating chevron
- **Keyboard friendly:** Collapsed panels can no longer be reached by tabbing, and keyboard focus returns to the Filters button when a panel closes
- **Profile save feedback:** The password page now confirms a successful change inline, explains failures without losing what was typed, and clears old messages as soon as editing resumes
- **Steady layout:** Reserved message space keeps the password fields and save button from jumping when validation messages appear or disappear

All icons across the attendance app move to a new two-tone icon set, drawn in the app's own colours, with small hover animations and clearer coloured accents on important actions.

- **Consistent two-tone look:** Every screen, button, menu, table action and map control now uses one matching icon family with a soft colour fill, replacing the previous mismatched outline icons
- **Icons that respond:** Key icons play a small motion when you hover or tap, such as a check ticking or a tab icon nudging, and respect the system setting for reduced motion
- **Meaningful colours:** Action icons carry meaning through colour: green for approvals, red for destructive actions like logging out, blue for profile, so risky taps stand out before they happen
- **Missing glyphs hand-drawn:** Arrows, check marks and a few control glyphs the set lacked were drawn in the same style, so nothing looks out of place

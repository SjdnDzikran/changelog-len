Attendance records and notifications become more informative and readable for everyone.

- **Selfie evidence at a glance:** Admin attendance lists now show check-in and check-out photo thumbnails right in the table, next to the punch times, so day-to-day oversight no longer requires opening each record to see the evidence.
- **Clearer punch layout:** Check-in and check-out are grouped into a single labeled column with the punctuality status (on time or late) directly beneath each time, and dates show the weekday underneath, making the tables read like a calendar.
- **Notifications in your language:** Approval and submission notifications (leave, permits, visits, work reports) are now stored as templates and rendered in the reader's chosen language, so Indonesian-speaking employees no longer receive English notifications.
- **Safer photo access:** Evidence photos are served through a protected, authenticated channel with permission checks per feature, replacing direct storage links. Someone with a photo link alone can no longer open another employee's evidence.

The tracking timeline in the profile portal now hides internal notes on shipment handling steps, matching the privacy cleanup on the main tracking site.

- **Internal notes hidden:** Manifest and delivery assignment steps no longer show notes with courier names, drivers, or vehicle details
- **Older records covered:** Entries stored before the server-side cleanup are also hidden from view
- **Privacy on every surface:** Customers get the same protected timeline whether they track from the main site or the portal

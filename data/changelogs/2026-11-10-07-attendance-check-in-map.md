The admin attendance dashboard now shows where people punched, not just when.

- **New check-in map:** The attendance trend line chart is replaced with a map showing each location's area and every check-in and check-out recorded with coordinates on the selected date; tap a point for the employee, time, distance from the location centre, and a Google Maps link
- **Problems surface first:** Punches recorded outside their location's allowed area are flagged in red and listed first, so anomalies are visible immediately without scrolling or clustering
- **Better screen layout:** Arrival times and status breakdown now share one row, and the map loads only when it comes into view so the dashboard stays fast

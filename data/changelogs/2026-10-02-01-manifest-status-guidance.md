The Update Status dialog in Live Monitoring now guides staff through each manifest stage instead of just asking for confirmation.

- **Where it is now:** Each dialog opens with a clear picture of the manifest's current position, so staff no longer have to guess whether a truck is still on the road or already arrived
- **What saving changes:** The dialog spells out exactly what happens when the status is saved, including automatic arrival timestamps, before anything is committed
- **What comes next:** Every dialog ends with the next operational step, such as creating a follow-up manifest for transit routes or preparing delivery sheets at the destination
- **Tailored per manifest type:** Transit handovers, destination arrivals and vendor handovers each get their own guidance, and vendor manifests now explain that parcels stay with the vendor for onward delivery

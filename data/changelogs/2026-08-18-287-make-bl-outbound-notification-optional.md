- **Optional notification:** The email recipient list (To/Cc) on BL Outbound submissions is no longer required. Users can now submit without selecting a recipient list, and the dropdown can be cleared after selection
- **Submit button always active:** The submit button is no longer blocked when no recipient list is chosen, removing a common point of friction for users who do not need email notifications on every outbound request

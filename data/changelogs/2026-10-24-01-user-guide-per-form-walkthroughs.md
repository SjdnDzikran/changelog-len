The LEN Approval user guide has been restructured into six complete form walkthroughs (BL Inbound, BL Outbound, Pickup Domestic Delivery, Pickup Delivery, MRN Request, and MRF Inbound). Each walkthrough now keeps everything for one task together: prerequisites, filled inputs, confirmation dialogs, notifications, follow-up actions, and the resulting status.

- **Complete walkthroughs:** Staff no longer need to jump between sections to follow a form from start to finish. Each of the six form chapters shows the full journey, from filling the form to the final notification and status, in one place.
- **WhatsApp price negotiation covered step by step:** The guide now shows how a Manager receives a price link on WhatsApp, enters the price on the opened page, and passes the task on for final approval. It also covers expired or completed links, escalation to the Director for prices above Rp50 million, and how to review estimates with incomplete reference data.
- **Shipment receipt (ACK) in the right place:** Proof-of-delivery receipt entry is now documented inside the relevant form's chapter, together with its reminder and confirmation messages, instead of as a separate detached section.
- **Fresh screenshots and a new Excel example:** All application screenshots were recaptured from the current released version, and a new MRF Inbound Excel example was added alongside the existing BL Inbound, Pickup Domestic, and CJ price examples.
- **Verified end to end:** All 159 guide pages were checked for overflow and broken images, with 183 application screenshots and 35 WhatsApp message illustrations verified, and all 68 existing pricing and negotiation tests passing.

The WMS web app has a completely rebuilt sign-in experience, giving the system a distinct new brand identity for everyone who opens it each morning.

- **New WMS logo and identity:** A purpose-designed warehouse mark (a warehouse with open doors over stock, carrying the LEN sign and the rising arrow from the company logo) is now the browser tab icon, the phone home-screen icon, and the header logo, with a special light-text version for dark mode so it stays readable in every theme
- **Fully redesigned login page:** A rich blue warehouse backdrop now carries the WMS logo that assembles itself on arrival, the company logo on a white tab, a live clock with a time-of-day greeting (Good morning / Good afternoon / Good evening), and a glass-styled sign-in card listing the four main modules: goods receiving, inventory, goods issuing, and delivery
- **Animated warehouse scene:** The bottom of the page now shows goods moving through a miniature warehouse, from the inbound truck through the scan gate and testing bench into the rack, then loaded by a forklift onto the outbound truck, complete with "Scanned", "Picked" and "Shipped" status badges. It scales to the screen size, disappears on phones, and stands still for users who prefer reduced motion
- **Smoother sign-in card:** Passwords can be revealed with an eye button, a warning appears when Caps Lock is on, and a "Remember my username" option (username only, never the password) fills the field on the next visit. A help tip points staff to their administrator for password resets

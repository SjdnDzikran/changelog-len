Employees who have not uploaded a photo now get a distinctive generated avatar instead of plain initials.

- **Unique per person:** Each employee is assigned their own colorful shape, generated from their account, so the same person always looks the same across the app
- **Seen everywhere:** The sidebar account menu, profile page, employee lists, and the late-ranking board all use the new avatars
- **Real photos still win:** Once an employee uploads a profile picture, it replaces the generated avatar automatically
- **No false circles:** The generated avatars keep their own organic shape instead of being forced into a round frame

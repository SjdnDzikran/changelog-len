- **Fixed unreadable account menu:** The profile and logout menu under your account name in the header was nearly invisible in some themes because it borrowed colors from the dark sidebar. It now has its own background and text colors that match the active theme.
- **Clearer menu actions:** The "Profil" and "Log out" options now have icons, comfortable touch-sized rows, and hover highlighting, so it is obvious what each option does.
- **Logout stands out:** The "Log out" option is shown in a distinct warning color, separating it from normal navigation.
- **Verified contrast:** Text in the menu meets readability standards in all four themes (Light, Dark, Cosmic, Corporate), in normal, hover, and pressed states, on both desktop and phone screen sizes.

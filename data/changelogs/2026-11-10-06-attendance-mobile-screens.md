The employee side of the attendance web app now looks and feels like a native mobile app instead of a shrunk-down desktop site.

- **App-style history:** Attendance history is a day-by-day list with colored date blocks and punch times instead of a spreadsheet-style table, plus a monthly summary up top showing present, late, leave and absent days
- **One-tap back:** The app bar's back arrow always returns to the previous screen in one press, no matter how many filters, months or detail views you opened along the way
- **Easier forms:** Employee and location pickers in admin forms are a single search-and-choose field instead of a separate search box plus dropdown; photo evidence for visits and overtime opens a full-screen camera with a "Use photo" confirmation
- **Request screens rebuilt:** Leave, permit, overtime, visit and work report pages use status filter chips, month-grouped lists, a floating new-request button, and a visual leave balance meter
- **Notifications and payroll as cards:** Notifications group by day (Today, Yesterday) with unread highlights; payroll shows one big net-pay figure with attendance tiles and an expandable calculation breakdown, and payslips as tappable rows

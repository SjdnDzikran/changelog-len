On the new DRS form, the save button now disables itself while a submission is in progress.

- **One click, one document:** Clicking "Simpan DRS" repeatedly no longer submits the same DRS multiple times; the button is inactive until the save finishes
- **No more duplicate cleanup:** Removes a common source of duplicated delivery sheets that staff had to find and delete afterwards
